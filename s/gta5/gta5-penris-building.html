Penris Building

📍 1000 Wilshire (Wedbush Center) · 1000 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90017 (between Wilshire Blvd and 7th St, beside the I-110)

1000 Wilshire (Wedbush Center) is a 21-storey postmodern office tower by KPF, opened in 1987, with rounded ends, beside the Harbor Freeway. GTA V makes it the Penris Building, home of the game's Apple-style tech giant, with a huge eye logo on top.
