Satellite Relay Station (Radio Telescope Array)

📍 Karl G. Jansky Very Large Array (VLA) — Visitor Center; alt.: Owens Valley Radio Observatory (CA) · VLA Visitor Center, Old Hwy 60 (off US-60), Magdalena, NM 87825

Swipe: game ⇄ real life

The VLA is an array of 27 movable 25 m radio dishes on the Plains of San Agustin in New Mexico, with a visitor center and a self-guided walk. GTA V's six-dish Satellite Relay Station in the Grand Senora Desert borrows its dish design.
