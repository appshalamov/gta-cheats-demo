Integrity Way and its tunnel

📍 3rd Street Tunnel (West 3rd Street under Bunker Hill) · W 3rd St tunnel, east portal at S Hill St (between 2nd & 3rd St), Los Angeles, CA 90013

The 3rd Street Tunnel carries W 3rd St under Bunker Hill. It opened in 1901 and was later lengthened and reworked, last in 1983-84. In GTA V this stretch of 3rd Street (Flower to Hill) becomes Integrity Way, which runs mostly in a tunnel linking Pillbox Hill and Textile City.
