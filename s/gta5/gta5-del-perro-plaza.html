Del Perro Plaza (mall)

📍 Strip mall at 11031–11049 Santa Monica Blvd ('Santa Monica Plaza') · 11031 Santa Monica Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90025

An ordinary strip mall on Santa Monica Boulevard in West LA. In GTA V it is reproduced as Del Perro Plaza, an outdoor mall with a food court.
