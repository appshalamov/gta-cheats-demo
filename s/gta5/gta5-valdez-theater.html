Valdez Theater

📍 Wiltern Theatre and Pellissier Building · 3790 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90010 (SE corner of Wilshire Blvd & Western Ave, Koreatown)

The 1931 Art Deco Wiltern Theatre and its 12-storey blue-green terracotta Pellissier Building tower at Wilshire & Western, now a concert venue. In GTA V it is the Valdez Theater at San Andreas Ave & Ginger St, with the same corner tower, vertical neon signs and marquee.
