Hawick replaces Melrose: a Scottish in-joke from Rockstar North

📍 Melrose Avenue · Melrose Avenue, район 7951 Melrose Ave (торговый участок между Fairfax и La Brea), Los Angeles, CA 90046

Hawick is based on LA's Melrose District, and the name swaps one Scottish Borders town for its neighbour. Melrose and Hawick are about 25 km apart, and Hawick is roughly 80 km south of Rockstar North's Edinburgh studio.
