Pescado Azul / Gutter & Blood building (Strawberry Ave)

📍 Bradbury Building · 304 S Broadway, Los Angeles, CA 90013

The Bradbury Building is an 1893 office block at Broadway & 3rd St, famous for its wrought-iron, skylit atrium. GTA V reuses its brick shell on Strawberry Ave, with the Pescado Azul restaurant and a Gutter & Blood boutique on the ground floor.
