La Mesa

📍 Arts District, Los Angeles — Southwestern Bag Co. warehouse · 1380 E 6th St / 635 Mateo St (6th & Mateo), Arts District, Los Angeles, CA 90021

The Arts District is a former rail-and-warehouse zone east of downtown LA, now lofts, breweries and galleries. GTA V turns its warehouse belt, plus bits of Boyle Heights and Vernon, into La Mesa, home of Lester's garment factory.
