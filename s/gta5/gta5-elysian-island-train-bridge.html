Elysian Island Train Bridge (Elysian Island Rail Bridge)

📍 Henry Ford Bridge (Badger Avenue Bridge, railroad lift bridge over the Cerritos Channel) · Cerritos Channel at Henry Ford Ave, between Wilmington and Terminal Island, Los Angeles, CA

The Henry Ford Bridge is a 1996 vertical-lift railroad bridge carrying the Pacific Harbor Line across the Cerritos Channel to Terminal Island. In GTA V it is the Elysian Island rail bridge between Banning and Elysian Island, next to the Signal Street Bridge.
