Richards Majestic Productions studio lot

📍 20th Century Studios lot (Fox Studios) · 10201 W Pico Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90064

The 20th Century Studios lot, the old Fox lot, sits beside Century City, which was built on its former backlot. In GTA V, Backlot City and the Richards Majestic studio stand in the same spot, borrowing details from Paramount and Sony.
