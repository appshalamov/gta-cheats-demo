Murrieta Oil Field

📍 Inglewood Oil Field (viewpoint: Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook) · Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook, 6300 Hetzler Rd, Culver City, CA 90232

The Inglewood Oil Field is a working urban oil field in the Baldwin Hills, with pumpjacks among the neighborhoods, best seen from the Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook. GTA V's Murrieta Oil Field in East Los Santos has the same kind of Brute pumpjacks, plus a refinery and a scrapyard.
