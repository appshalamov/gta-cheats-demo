De Santa Residence (Michael's house)

📍 Spanish Revival mansions of the Beverly Hills flats (N Bedford Dr / N Alpine Dr) · 800 block of N Bedford Dr (also 600 block of N Alpine Dr), Beverly Hills, CA 90210. Private homes, street level only.

These are typical Spanish Revival mansions in the Beverly Hills flats. Michael De Santa's house in GTA V is a collective image of them, not a copy of one specific house.
