Olympic Freeway x La Puerta Freeway stack

📍 Judge Harry Pregerson Interchange (I-105 / I-110) · I-105 / I-110 interchange (Harbor Freeway C Line station), Athens / Watts, Los Angeles, CA 90061

The Judge Harry Pregerson Interchange is a five-level stack joining I-105 and I-110, with Metro C Line tracks and a station in its middle. In GTA V the comparable junction is where the Olympic Freeway (I-4) meets the La Puerta Freeway (I-5).
