Dutch London Street Bridge (El Rancho Boulevard Bridge)

📍 First Street Viaduct (1st Street Bridge) · E 1st St over the Los Angeles River, between Santa Fe Ave and Mission Rd, Los Angeles, CA

The First Street Viaduct crosses the LA River near Little Tokyo, with Neoclassical triumphal-arch pylons at its corners and Metro E Line trains down the middle. GTA V moves its copy south, to the El Rancho Blvd / Dutch London St crossing between Rancho and Cypress Flats.
