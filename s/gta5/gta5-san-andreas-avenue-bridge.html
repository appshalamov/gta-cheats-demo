San Andreas Avenue Bridge

📍 Sixth Street Viaduct (original 1932 bridge, demolished 2016; replaced 2022) · 6th St / Whittier Blvd over the Los Angeles River, between the Arts District and Boyle Heights, Los Angeles, CA

The 1932 Sixth Street Viaduct was the iconic twin-steel-arch bridge over the concrete LA River between the Arts District and Boyle Heights. GTA V keeps it as the San Andreas Avenue Bridge, but the real one was demolished in 2016 and replaced by a new viaduct in 2022.
