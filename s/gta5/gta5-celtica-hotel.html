The Celtica Hotel (Los Santos)

📍 Chester Williams Building · 452 S Broadway / 215 W 5th St, Los Angeles, CA 90013 (NE corner of 5th St & Broadway)

The Chester Williams Building is a 12-storey Beaux-Arts highrise from 1926 at 5th and Broadway in LA's Historic Core. In GTA V it is the Los Santos branch of The Celtica Hotel, with a Crucial Fix cafe on the ground floor, at San Andreas Ave and Strawberry Ave.
