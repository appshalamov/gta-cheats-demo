Signal Street Bridge

📍 Commodore Schuyler F. Heim Bridge (former SR-47 vertical-lift bridge over the Cerritos Channel) · SR-47 (Terminal Island Fwy) over the Cerritos Channel, Wilmington / Terminal Island, Los Angeles, CA

The Heim Bridge was a 1948 vertical-lift bridge carrying SR-47 over the Cerritos Channel. It was decommissioned in October 2015, and a fixed span completed in September 2020 replaced it. GTA V recreates the old lift bridge as the Signal Street Bridge, which cannot lift.
