Mile High Club (construction site)

📍 The Ritz-Carlton / JW Marriott tower at L.A. LIVE · 900 W Olympic Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90015

The 54-storey JW Marriott / Ritz-Carlton tower, designed by Gensler and opened in 2010, anchors the L.A. LIVE district next to Crypto.com Arena. In GTA V it is the Mile High Club, an unfinished skyscraper with a climbable crane and parachute jumps.
