Maze Bank Arena

📍 Kia Forum (formerly the Great Western Forum / 'The Fabulous Forum') · 3900 W Manchester Blvd, Inglewood, CA 90305

The Kia Forum is a 1967 indoor arena in Inglewood, near LAX, and the former home of the Lakers and Kings. San Andreas turned it into the Los Santos Forum, and GTA V renamed it the bank-sponsored Maze Bank Arena next to the airport, but gave it a Coliseum-style exterior.
