Vinewood Wax Haven's celebrity murals copy the Hollywood Wax Museum

📍 Hollywood Wax Museum · Hollywood Wax Museum, 6767 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028 (сам музей платный, фасад смотреть бесплатно)

The Vinewood Wax Haven entrance shows look-alikes of Marilyn Monroe, Charlie Chaplin, John Wayne and James Dean, and a bigger mural of the same four covers the back wall. The real Hollywood Wax Museum had celebrity murals in both spots, the rear one visible from N Highland Ave.
