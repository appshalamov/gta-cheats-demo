Templar Hotel

📍 Hotel Rosslyn (1914) and Hotel Rosslyn Annex (1923) · 5th St & Main St: Annex 112 W 5th St; original Rosslyn 451 S Main St, Los Angeles, CA 90013

The Hotel Rosslyn and its Annex are twin Beaux-Arts hotels by John Parkinson at 5th and Main, famous for their huge heart-shaped 'Hotel Rosslyn' rooftop signs. GTA V turns them into the two Templar Hotel buildings southeast of Legion Square.
