Maze Bank Arena's bank naming echoes the Great Western Forum

📍 Kia Forum · Kia Forum, 3900 W Manchester Blvd, Inglewood, CA 90305

The arena carries a bank's name. The Forum in Inglewood did the same as the 'Great Western Forum' (Great Western Bank naming rights, 1988–2003); it is now the Kia Forum. The Forum was also the model for GTA San Andreas' Los Santos Forum, which GTA Wiki treats as the predecessor of the Maze Bank Arena.
