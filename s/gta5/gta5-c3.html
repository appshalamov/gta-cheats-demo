Los Santos County Coroner Office

📍 Los Angeles County Department of Medical Examiner (historic Coroner building) · 1104 N Mission Rd, Los Angeles, CA 90033

This is the historic red-brick headquarters of LA County's medical examiner, renamed from Coroner in 2023, on N Mission Rd opposite the county hospital campus. In GTA V it is the Los Santos County Coroner Office, which Michael infiltrates in 'Dead Man Walking'.
