Little Seoul Tower

📍 Equitable Life Building (Equitable Plaza) · 3435 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90010 (just east of Normandie Ave, Koreatown)

A 34-storey, 138 m office tower from 1969 with white vertical fins, designed by Welton Becket's firm. In GTA V it is Little Seoul Tower, the tallest building in Little Seoul, home to Total Bankers and the Hit 'n' Run coffee shop.
