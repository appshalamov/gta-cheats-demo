Weazel Plaza Shootout = Die Hard at Nakatomi Plaza (Fox Plaza)

📍 Fox Plaza · Fox Plaza, 2121 Avenue of the Stars, Century City, Los Angeles, CA 90067 (office tower, view from the street)

In a Christmas-season GTA Online random event (Festive Surprise 2022–2025), robbers fight an LSPD helicopter on the roof of Weazel Plaza. The tower is GTA's copy of Fox Plaza, which played Nakatomi Plaza in Die Hard (1988), and Weazel is GTA's parody of Fox. Two robbers are lookalikes of Hans Gruber and Karl.
