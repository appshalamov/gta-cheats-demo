Schlongberg Sachs: a Goldman Sachs parody in a real LA tower

📍 FourFortyFour South Flower · FourFortyFour South Flower, 444 S Flower St, Los Angeles, CA 90071

The 48-storey, 191 m tower at 444 S Flower St (formerly Citigroup Center, now FourFortyFour South Flower) appears in Pillbox Hill as the 'Schlongberg Sachs Center'. Both Wikipedia and GTA Wiki describe it as a parody of Goldman Sachs, with Yiddish slang in the name.
