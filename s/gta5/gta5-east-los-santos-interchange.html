📍 East Los Angeles Interchange (I-5 / I-10 / US-101 / SR-60) · Boyle Heights, Los Angeles, CA 90023/90033

The East Los Angeles Interchange in Boyle Heights joins I-5, I-10, US-101 and SR-60 and is among the busiest interchanges in the world. In GTA V it corresponds to the freeway tangle in East Los Santos, east of the LS River.
