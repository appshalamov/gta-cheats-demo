Vespucci Boulevard Bridge

📍 Fourth Street Viaduct (4th Street Bridge) · E 4th St over the Los Angeles River, between the Arts District and Boyle Heights, Los Angeles, CA

The Fourth Street Viaduct crosses the LA River with Gothic Revival pylons and pointed arches, linking the Arts District and Boyle Heights. In GTA V it is the Vespucci Boulevard Bridge between Mission Row and La Mesa, with the same Gothic towers over the channel.
