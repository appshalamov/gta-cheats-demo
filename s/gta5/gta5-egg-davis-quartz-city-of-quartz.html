Davis Quartz probably nods to Mike Davis's 'City of Quartz'

The quarry's name probably plays on City of Quartz: Excavating the Future in Los Angeles (1990) by historian Mike Davis. GTA Wiki says the quarry itself looks like the Eagle Mountain iron mine on the east edge of Joshua Tree, which the book also mentions.
