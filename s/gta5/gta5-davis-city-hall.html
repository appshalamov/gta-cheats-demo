Davis City Hall

📍 Compton City Hall (Compton Civic Center) · 205 S Willowbrook Ave, Compton, CA 90220

Compton City Hall, completed in 1976 by Harold L. Williams, is a low Late Modern building with huge vertical concrete fins on the Civic Center plaza. GTA V's Davis City Hall copies it, together with the courthouse tower and the MLK monument beside it.
