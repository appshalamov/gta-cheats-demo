The 1966 Digestive Co.

📍 Biscuit Company Lofts (former National Biscuit Company / Nabisco West Coast HQ) · 1850 Industrial St, Los Angeles, CA 90021 (corner of Industrial St & Mateo St, Arts District)

A 1925 seven-storey Nabisco factory in the Arts District, converted into 104 live/work lofts in 2006. In GTA V it appears as the brick 'The 1966 Digestive Co.' factory on Popular St in La Mesa, beside the river bridges.
