GTA V preserves two LA bridges that no longer exist

📍 Sixth Street Viaduct · Sixth Street Viaduct, 6th St over the LA River, Los Angeles, CA 90021; Heim Bridge, SR-47 over the Cerritos Channel, Terminal Island

The San Andreas Avenue Bridge copies the original 1932 Sixth Street Viaduct, demolished in 2016; a new viaduct opened on the same spot in July 2022 and is open to pedestrians.
