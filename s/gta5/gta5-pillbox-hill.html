Pillbox Hill

📍 Bunker Hill (Downtown Los Angeles) · Bunker Hill, Downtown Los Angeles, CA 90071 (Grand Ave / California Plaza)

Bunker Hill is downtown LA's high-rise financial district on a hill above the old city. GTA V turns it into Pillbox Hill, Los Santos's skyscraper core with the Maze Bank Tower and the FIB building.
