Robert Dazzler International Jewelry Exchange

📍 International Jewelry Center · 550 S Hill St, Los Angeles, CA 90013

The International Jewelry Center is a 16-storey jewelry trade tower from 1981 in the Jewelry District, facing Pershing Square across Hill St. In GTA V it appears, much shortened, as the Robert Dazzler International Jewelry Exchange.
