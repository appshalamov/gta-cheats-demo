Bayview Lodge lumberjack mirrors the giant smuggler at Blackgang Chine

📍 Blackgang Chine theme park entrance · Blackgang Chine theme park entrance, Blackgang Rd, Blackgang, Isle of Wight, UK

The giant Logger Beer lumberjack at Bayview Lodge carries a keg on his raised shoulder. It strikes the same pose as the giant smuggler with a branded barrel at the entrance of Blackgang Chine theme park on the Isle of Wight, England, which GTA Wiki names as the model. Shooting the in-game keg makes beer pour out.
