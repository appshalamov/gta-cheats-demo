Del Perro Beach Lifeguard Station

📍 Santa Monica Lifeguard Headquarters (LA County Lifeguards) · 1642 Ocean Front Walk, Santa Monica, CA 90401

The 1950s LA County lifeguard headquarters by Welton Becket & Associates, on the beach just south of Santa Monica Pier. In GTA V it is the Del Perro Beach Lifeguard Station next to the pier's south parking lot.
