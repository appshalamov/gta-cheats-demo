Port Gellhorn (name origin)

📍 Captain Tony's Saloon (original Sloppy Joe's site, 1933-1937) · 428 Greene St, Key West, FL 33040

Open in Secret Base

Captain Tony's Saloon occupies the original Sloppy Joe's building, where war correspondent Martha Gellhorn met Ernest Hemingway in December 1936. Fans think GTA VI's Port Gellhorn may be named after her, but the town itself is modeled on the Panhandle (Panama City).
