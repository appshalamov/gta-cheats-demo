Unnamed waterfront grandstand on Gloriana Key

📍 Miami Marine Stadium · 3501 Rickenbacker Cswy, Miami, FL 33149

Miami Marine Stadium is a 1963 concrete grandstand on Virginia Key with a huge cantilevered folded-plate roof, facing a rowing and powerboat basin. It has been abandoned since 1992. GTADB places a similar waterfront grandstand on the game's Gloriana Key.
