Vice City branding on real Miami Beach chairs and umbrellas (pending)

📍 South Beach beach behind Lummus Park · South Beach beach behind Lummus Park, Ocean Dr (5th–15th St), Miami Beach, FL 33139

On Sept 10, 2026 the Miami Beach City Commission voted 4-3 to authorize negotiations with Rockstar/Take-Two over a roughly $3-4M deal. It would put discreet 'Vice City' / 'VI' branding on privately run beach chairs and umbrellas from Oct 15 to Dec 31, 2026, with nothing on lifeguard stands or city landmarks.
