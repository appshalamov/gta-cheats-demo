Bocamar Bridge

📍 Bob Graham Sunshine Skyway Bridge (I-275 over Tampa Bay) · I-275 across the mouth of Tampa Bay, between St. Petersburg (Pinellas Co.) and Terra Ceia (Manatee Co.), FL

Florida's signature cable-stayed bridge across the mouth of Tampa Bay, with two pylons and a single plane of yellow stay cables. In GTA VI it appears as the Bocamar Bridge near Port Gellhorn, glimpsed in the distance in Trailer 2.
