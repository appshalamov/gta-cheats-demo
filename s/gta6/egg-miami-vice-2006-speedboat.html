Speedboats racing past the port: a Miami Vice homage

📍 PortMiami container terminal · PortMiami container terminal, gantry cranes on Fisherman's Channel, Dodge Island, Miami, FL (restricted; view from the water)

Powerboats, including a Cigarette Top Gun-style racer, tear through the bay past the container ship BTN Delmar under the cranes of PortViceCity. GTA Wiki calls it a direct reference to Michael Mann's Miami Vice (2006), and Dexerto compares it to the Miami Vice opening credits.
