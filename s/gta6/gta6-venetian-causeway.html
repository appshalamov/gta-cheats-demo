Rialto Causeway / Rialto Islands (VicePass toll plaza, Rivo Bridge)

📍 Venetian Causeway: Venetian Islands, toll plaza on Biscayne Island and West Venetian Causeway Bridge · Venetian Causeway, from NE 15th St / Biscayne Blvd (Miami) to Dade Blvd (Miami Beach); toll plaza on Biscayne Island

The Venetian Causeway (1926) island-hops over the man-made Venetian Islands between Miami and Miami Beach, with two bascule bridges and a toll plaza on Biscayne Island. In GTA VI it becomes the Rialto Causeway and Rialto Islands, with a VicePass toll plaza and the Rivo Bridge drawbridge.
