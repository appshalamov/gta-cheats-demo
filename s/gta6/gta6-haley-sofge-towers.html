📍 Haley Sofge Towers (public housing) · 750 & 800 NW 13th Ave, Miami, FL 33125

Haley Sofge Towers is a 1970s public-housing complex of mid-rise towers on NW 13th Ave in Riverside. In GTA VI it is the Venture Apartments, where Lucia escapes through a window in Trailer 2 and where the Extended Look's raid mission takes place.
