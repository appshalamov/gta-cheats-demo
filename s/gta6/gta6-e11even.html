E11EVEN is a 24/7 downtown Miami nightclub and cabaret known for its rooftop sign and for banner planes advertising over South Beach. GTA VI parodies it as NINE1NINE, with a banner plane over Vice Beach in Trailer 1 and the club where Jason and Lucia dance in Trailer 2.
