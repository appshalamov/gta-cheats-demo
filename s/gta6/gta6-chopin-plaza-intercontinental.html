Clarissa Hotel and downtown bayfront road (Trailer 2 night truck scene)

📍 InterContinental Miami, 100 Chopin Plaza · 100 Chopin Plaza, Miami, FL 33131

The InterContinental Miami is a 1980s downtown bayfront hotel next to Bayfront Park. In the Trailer 2 night scene where Lucia climbs onto a truck, it and its neighbouring towers appear beside an invented highway, and GTADB calls the in-game hotel 'Clarissa Hotel'.
