Firefly Plaza

📍 Helen Mar (Art Deco apartment building, formerly 'The Pancoast Plaza') · 2421 Lake Pancoast Dr, Miami Beach, FL 33140

Helen Mar is a 1936 Art Deco apartment tower (now condos) on Lake Pancoast Drive by Collins Canal, topped by a rooftop tower carrying its name. In GTA VI it is Firefly Plaza in Washington Beach, with 'FIREFLY PLAZA' on the same kind of rooftop tower in the official Vice City screenshot.
