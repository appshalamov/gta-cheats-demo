Vice City Arts Center: Galina Ballet Opera House, Vice City Concert Hall, Palaestra Tower

📍 Adrienne Arsht Center for the Performing Arts (Ziff Ballet Opera House, Knight Concert Hall, 1929 Sears Tower) · 1300 Biscayne Blvd, Miami, FL 33132

Miami's performing arts complex (César Pelli, 2006): Ziff Ballet Opera House and Knight Concert Hall on either side of Biscayne Blvd, plus the 1929 Art Deco Sears tower. In GTA VI it is the Vice City Arts Center: Galina Ballet Opera House, Vice City Concert Hall and Palaestra Tower.
