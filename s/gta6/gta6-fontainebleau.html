📍 Trésor Tower penthouse, Fontainebleau Miami Beach · 4401 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33140 (main hotel entrance: 4441 Collins Ave)

The Fontainebleau is Morris Lapidus's 1954 resort hotel. Its 37-storey Trésor Tower has a top-floor penthouse with a private pool on a wraparound terrace. Fans matched the rooftop pool party in Trailer 1 (woman in the white bikini) to that penthouse.
