Blue-glass rounded condo tower in the opening shot

📍 1500 Ocean Drive (condominium) · 1500 Ocean Dr, Miami Beach, FL 33139

1500 Ocean Drive is a condo tower at the north end of Ocean Drive, with a rounded blue-glass corner cylinder topped by columns and a pink slab beside it. In GTA VI it appears in Trailer 1's opening Vice Beach shot and again behind the beach crowd in the official 'Vice City' screenshot 05.
