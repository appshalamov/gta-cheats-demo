Effluvia

📍 Juvia rooftop (closed 2023), penthouse of 1111 Lincoln Road · 1111 Lincoln Rd, Miami Beach, FL 33139

Juvia was a rooftop restaurant and lounge atop the 1111 Lincoln Road office building, next to the Herzog & de Meuron garage. It closed in 2023. In GTA VI it becomes Effluvia, the rooftop bar where Jason and Lucia meet Brian and Lori Heder.
