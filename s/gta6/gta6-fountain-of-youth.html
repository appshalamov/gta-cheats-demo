State of Leonida (name from Ponce de Leon)

📍 Ponce de Leon's Fountain of Youth Archaeological Park · 11 Magnolia Ave, St. Augustine, FL 32084

The Fountain of Youth park in St. Augustine is tied to the legend of Juan Ponce de León, who led the first European expedition to Florida in 1513. GTA VI's state of Leonida is widely read as a play on his name.
