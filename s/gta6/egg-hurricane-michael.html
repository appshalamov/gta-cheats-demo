Port Gellhorn frozen around Hurricane Michael (2018)

Parts of Port Gellhorn copy Callaway (Bay County, next to Panama City) as it looked before Hurricane Michael hit on Oct 10, 2018. Crossroad Park Minimall in Trailer 2 recreates Callaway Village Square, a strip mall that was demolished after the storm; a CEFCO now stands on the site.
