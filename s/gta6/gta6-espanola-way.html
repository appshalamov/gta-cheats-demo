Paño Rojo (Spanish-style pedestrian street)

📍 Española Way — Tapas y Tintos (448 Española Way) · 448 Española Way, Miami Beach, FL 33139

Española Way is a short Mediterranean Revival pedestrian street in South Beach lined with restaurants. In GTA VI, Lucia stands outside 'Paño Rojo', a copy of the Tapas y Tintos tapas bar at 448 Española Way.
