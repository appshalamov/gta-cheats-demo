📍 Trafalgar Road at Iroquois Shore Road, Oakville (naked-man police chase, Oct 2022) · Trafalgar Rd & Iroquois Shore Rd, Oakville, ON L6H, Canada

A busy Oakville arterial next to the QEW where, on Oct 12, 2022, a naked man fleeing Halton police ran south down Trafalgar Road. Phone video of the chase went viral and is widely cited as the source of a GTA VI Trailer 1 clip.
