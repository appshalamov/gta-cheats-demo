Crossroad Park Minimall (Uncle Jack's Liquor, Happy Nails, Whiz)

📍 Former Callaway Village Square strip mall (demolished after Hurricane Michael; the site is now a Casey's gas station, formerly CEFCO) · 219–243 N Tyndall Pkwy (US-98), Callaway, FL 32404

A strip mall on Tyndall Parkway in Callaway, wrecked by Hurricane Michael in 2018 and replaced by a gas station. In GTA VI it survives as Crossroad Park Minimall on Interstate 404, home of Uncle Jack's Liquor, seen in Trailer 2 at 2:28.
