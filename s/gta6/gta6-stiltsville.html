📍 Stiltsville, Biscayne National Park · Biscayne Bay, ~1.5 km south of Cape Florida, Key Biscayne, FL 33149

Stiltsville is a group of wooden houses on stilts in Biscayne Bay, about 1.5 km south of Cape Florida, now part of Biscayne National Park. GTA VI has similar stilt houses in open water south of Catalan Key, seen in an official Leonida Keys screenshot.
