Yellow-and-white 1950s car on Shore Dr (Avalon nod)

📍 Avalon Hotel and its yellow-and-white Oldsmobile · 700 Ocean Dr, Miami Beach, FL 33139

The Avalon is an Art Deco hotel at Ocean Drive and 7th St that keeps a yellow-and-white 1950s Oldsmobile parked out front as a photo prop. Trailer 1 of GTA VI shows a similar two-tone classic car on Shore Dr, which some outlets read as a nod to it.
